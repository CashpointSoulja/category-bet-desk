# v2 roadmap

| Priority | Item | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Live pilot feed.** Read orders, repeat purchases, disputes and double-graded samples for a bet's suppliers and fill the scorecard automatically, with sample sizes. | Removes manual entry and makes the gate call reproducible. |
| 2 | **Threshold lock and versioning.** Lock scale/kill lines at pilot start; any change creates a new version, voids earlier calls and shows on the memo. | Stops quiet goalpost moves. |
| 3 | **Portfolio view.** All bets side by side: gate, GMV vs £5M, contribution, placeholders left. | The role owns a portfolio, not one bet. |
| 4 | **Seasonality and cohorts.** Monthly demand curves (outerwear in autumn) and buyer cohorts instead of one retention rate. | Run-rate in a peak month overstates annual GMV. |
| 5 | **Lane-level freight.** Freight and duty per origin hub and destination market, from forwarder quotes. | Freight is the second-largest swing in the sensitivity table. |
| 6 | **Supplier concentration check.** Hold the gate if one supplier carries most of the GMV. | A bet that depends on one supplier is not proven. |
| 7 | **Grade calibration view.** Confusion matrix of FleekSort vs QC grades per category. | Shows which grade boundary causes disputes. |
| 8 | **Shareable memo link** and PDF export without the print dialog. | Easier to circulate before a decision meeting. |
| 9 | **Currency.** Work in GBP, USD or EUR with a dated rate. | Listings show USD to some visitors. |

Not planned: buyer- or supplier-level data in the memo, and automatic scale decisions. The desk recommends; the bet owner decides.
