# Viability memo: Category Bet Desk and the Special Projects Lead role

Independent concept by Ayomide Ahmed. Not an official Fleek product. Uses public information only.

## The role, from public sources

The [job post](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fleek/jobs/Iopbt9d-special-projects-lead-category-expansion) describes a London-based owner who takes "new category bets from thesis to proven economics", each of which "could add £5M+ in GMV", taking each from thesis to first suppliers, first GMV and a feasible-or-not call, with win and kill metrics agreed up front. Fleek's public scale: about 2,000 verified suppliers and more than 50,000 buyers in 100+ countries ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/08/fleek-an-online-marketplace-connecting-vintage-clothing-wholesalers-and-retailers-raises-25-million-in-new-funding/), July 2026; the home page says 45,000+ buyers), a $25M Series B, and FleekSort, its grading system for secondhand clothing ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/fleek-25m-series-b-secondhand-fashion-ai); [careers page](https://www.joinfleek.com/careers), which cites about 82% accuracy).

## Why a desk, and why for this seat

- **The job is a portfolio, so bets need the same yardstick.** The desk gives each bet the same P&L, ramp and gates, so a denim bet and a kidswear bet can be compared directly.
- **£5M GMV is a scale test, not a margin test.** On the preloaded estimates all three bets earn a similar contribution per bundle (about £18-22), but the ramp splits them: sportswear reaches a £5M run-rate in month 17, denim in month 15, and kidswear does not reach it within 24 months because its low price per piece needs far more bundles. That is the kind of difference the role has to surface early.
- **Grading is Fleek's edge and its risk.** FleekSort makes graded, searchable bundles possible, but buyer reviews on [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/joinfleek.com) (4.4 from 1,233 reviews) include complaints about grade and condition mismatches. The desk makes grading agreement and dispute rate gate metrics, so a category is not scaled until its grades hold up.
- **Kill discipline is in the brief.** Thresholds and minimum samples are set first; missing or thin evidence holds; any kill on an adequate sample ends the bet.

## What it would take to use for real

1. Replace the 11 placeholders per bet with Fleek's real take rate, CAC, freight, grading cost and dispute data (internal, so not in this concept).
2. Feed pilot readings from order and dispute data instead of manual entry.
3. Agree default thresholds per category with the founders.

## Cost and risk

The desk is a static site with no back end; v1 costs nothing to run. The main risk is false confidence from a simple calculation, which the labels, sensitivity tables and gate refusals are there to counter.

## Recommendation

Use the desk as the standard case file for every category bet: one page per bet, reviewed at each gate. Start with whichever bet has the most placeholders already replaced by supplier quotes.
