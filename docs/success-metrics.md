# Success metrics

How we would judge the desk if it were used for real. Targets are proposals, not benchmarks.

## North star

**Share of category bets that reach a recorded scale or kill call within 120 days of their thesis being written.** Target: 80%. A bet stuck on HOLD past 120 days is the failure this tool exists to prevent.

## Input metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Thresholds set before pilot | Bets whose scale/kill lines were saved before the first pilot order ÷ bets with a pilot | 100% |
| Placeholder burn-down | Placeholder inputs remaining when the gate is called | ≤ 3 of 29 |
| Memo used in decision | Scale/kill meetings that used the exported memo ÷ all such meetings | ≥ 90% |
| Time to first economics | Days from idea to a first full bundle P&L | ≤ 2 days |

## Outcome metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Call accuracy | Scaled bets that are still above their kill lines 6 months later ÷ scaled bets | ≥ 75% |
| Cost of a kill | Spend and team weeks on a bet before a kill call | Trending down |
| Ramp forecast error | Actual month-6 GMV vs the desk's month-6 forecast at scale call | Within ±30% |

## Guardrails

- No scale call on a metric below its minimum sample (enforced by the gate).
- Dispute rate on scaled bets stays under its kill line.
- No real buyer- or supplier-level data in the memo.

## What the demo itself proves

The demo cannot show outcomes. It shows the mechanics: labels on every input, a gate that holds on missing evidence, and the same input producing the same memo. Those are checked in the [test plan](test-plan.md).
