1Thesis: Vintage sportswear & branded outdoor

Who buys, who supplies, and why Fleek is placed to win this category.

Starting point. Fleek already lists this stock: Nike track pants, The North Face fleece and puffers, Columbia fleece and jackets. The bet is a dedicated £5M line with new supply behind it, not a category launch from zero. Sourced ↗

Who buys

  • Online resellers on eBay, Vinted and Depop, plus vintage shops. The home page quotes buyers from these channels next to "45,000+ buyers source on Fleek".Sourced ↗
  • Seasonal outerwear buyers who stock up before autumn and winter.Estimated

Who supplies

  • Graders and rag houses, mostly in India, Pakistan and Dubai, where much of the world’s secondhand clothing is sorted.Sourced ↗
  • Branded outerwear surfacing from EU separate textile collection, which Fortune reports became mandatory last year.EstimatedFortune, 8 Jul 2026: Fleek raises $25M Series B

Why Fleek wins

  • Demand already sits on Fleek, so this is a supply-first test rather than a two-sided cold start.Sourced ↗
  • Brand recognition and authenticity scoring from a photo matter most for branded goods, and FleekSort does both.Sourced ↗
  • Logos make the stock easy to search, so it should sell through faster than unbranded mixes.Estimated

Vintage sportswear & branded outdoor

Gate call: HOLD Repeat-buyer rate (90 days): not measured yet. Dispute rate: not measured yet. Contribution per bundle: not measured yet. Grading agreement (FleekSort vs QC): not measured yet.

Thesis

Who buys. Online resellers on eBay, Vinted and Depop, plus vintage shops. The home page quotes buyers from these channels next to "45,000+ buyers source on Fleek". Seasonal outerwear buyers who stock up before autumn and winter.
Who supplies. Graders and rag houses, mostly in India, Pakistan and Dubai, where much of the world’s secondhand clothing is sorted. Branded outerwear surfacing from EU separate textile collection, which Fortune reports became mandatory last year.
Why Fleek wins. Demand already sits on Fleek, so this is a supply-first test rather than a two-sided cold start. Brand recognition and authenticity scoring from a photo matter most for branded goods, and FleekSort does both. Logos make the stock easy to search, so it should sell through faster than unbranded mixes.
